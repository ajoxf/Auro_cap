FAQ

Common questions.

How long does account verification take?
Verification is typically completed within 30 minutes during business hours. Submit a clear government-issued ID and a recent proof of address — we'll notify you by email the moment your account is active.
What deposit and withdrawal methods are supported?
Bank transfer, Visa & Mastercard, Skrill, Neteller, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and major crypto rails. Auro covers all transaction fees on deposits.
Are deposits and withdrawals free?
Yes — Auro Brokers does not charge any internal fees on deposits or withdrawals. Some payment providers may apply their own charges; we always disclose these before you confirm.
How long do withdrawals take?
Card and e-wallet withdrawals are processed within 4 hours during business hours. Bank wires typically settle within 1–3 business days.
Is my money safe with Auro Brokers?
All client funds are segregated in tier-1 banks and protected under FCA, CySEC and FSCA frameworks. Negative balance protection is included on every account.
Can I have multiple accounts?
Yes — you can hold up to 10 sub-accounts under a single login, including a mix of live and demo, in any of our 5 base currencies.
Do you offer a swap-free Islamic account?
Yes. Our Islamic account is fully Shariah-compliant — no overnight swap or rollover interest is charged on positions held overnight.
What is negative balance protection?
In the event of extreme market volatility, you cannot lose more than your account balance. Auro absorbs any negative balance — guaranteed across every account type.

GLOSSARY

Speak the markets.

Ask

The price at which the market is willing to sell a currency pair to you. Always slightly higher than the bid — the difference is the spread.

Bid

The price at which the market is willing to buy a currency pair from you. Quoted alongside the ask in every two-way market.

CFD

Contract for Difference — a derivative that lets you speculate on the price movement of an asset without owning the underlying.

Drawdown

The peak-to-trough decline in your account balance during a specific period. A core measure of risk in any trading strategy.

ECN

Electronic Communication Network — a venue that aggregates liquidity from multiple banks and prime brokers, with no dealing desk.

Forex

The foreign exchange market — the largest, most liquid market on earth, with daily turnover exceeding $7 trillion.

Hedging

Holding offsetting positions in correlated instruments to reduce net exposure to a particular price move or risk factor.

Leverage

The ability to control a larger position with a smaller deposit. Amplifies both gains and losses — used judiciously.

Pip

The smallest standard price increment on a forex pair — typically the fourth decimal place (or second, for JPY pairs).

Spread

The difference between the bid and ask price. The smaller the spread, the lower the cost of entering and exiting trades.
